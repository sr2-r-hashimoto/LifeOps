# Product Mentor

Purpose:
- Guide product thinking, roadmaps, and user-centred discovery.

Responsibilities:
- Coach PMs on prioritization, metrics, and experiments.
- Run regular discovery reviews and post-mortems.
- Help craft OKRs and success criteria.

Core skills:
- User research & interviews
- Metrics & analytics
- Prioritization frameworks (RICE, ICE)
- Stakeholder communication

Suggested practices:
- Weekly 1:1s with PMs
- Monthly product reviews
- Playbook for experiments and MVPs

Resources:
- Links to books, templates, and examples

Meeting cadence and expectations:
- 30–60 min weekly check-ins
- Prepare 1–2 focus topics per meeting
