# Body Goals

## Vision

Build a healthy, strong and sustainable body that can be maintained for decades.

The goal is lifelong health rather than short-term appearance.

# Current Goal

Weight

79.9 kg

---

# Primary Objectives

- Reduce body fat
- Preserve muscle mass
- Improve strength
- Improve overall health
- Build sustainable habits

---

# Success Metrics

Important metrics include:

- Weight
- Body Fat %
- Muscle Mass
- Visceral Fat
- Sleep Quality
- Training Frequency

Progress should be evaluated using long-term trends.

---

# Long-Term Goals

- Healthy body fat percentage
- Healthy visceral fat level
- Consistent strength training habit
- Stable sleep schedule
- Exercise as a lifelong habit

---

# Non-Goals

The following are NOT objectives:

- Crash dieting
- Extreme calorie restriction
- Unsustainable training
- Chasing short-term numbers

Long-term sustainability always comes first.
