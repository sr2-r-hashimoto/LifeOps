# Body Plan

## Current Phase

Phase 1 - Fat Loss Foundation

The primary objective is to establish sustainable habits while reducing body fat and preserving muscle mass.

This phase prioritizes consistency over speed.

---

# Strategy

Current strategy:

- Daily calorie target: 1700 kcal
- High-protein diet
- Full-body resistance training
- Morning gym routine
- Progressive overload
- Consistent sleep schedule

Every decision should support long-term adherence.

---

# Weekly Targets

Training

- Full-body training 3 times per week

Nutrition

- Stay close to the calorie target
- Prioritize protein intake
- Minimize unnecessary liquid calories

Recovery

- Sleep around 22:00
- Wake around 05:30
- Maintain consistent wake-up times

# Success Criteria

This phase is considered successful when:

- Gym attendance is consistent.
- Protein intake is consistently high.
- Weight decreases gradually.
- Muscle mass is maintained.
- Healthy habits become automatic.

Rapid weight loss is not the objective.

Long-term consistency is.
